📖 Financial Glossary & Filing Categories
Headline P&L, Balance Sheet & Cash Terms
RevenueTop-line total sales from goods/services before any expenses.
EBITDA & OPM %Operating profit before interest, tax, depreciation & amortisation. Core operating margin proxy.
PBT & PATProfit Before Tax and Profit After Tax (bottom-line net profit attributable to shareholders).
QoQ / YoYQuarter-on-Quarter (vs immediate prior quarter) and Year-on-Year (vs same quarter a year ago, stripping seasonality).
Cash Conversion (CFO/PAT)Operating cash flow divided by net profit. Persistent PAT without matching CFO (<75%) is an accounting quality red flag.
Working Capital Cycle (CCC)Debtor Days + Inventory Days − Payable Days. A lengthening cycle quietly drains cash.
ROCE / ROE / D/EReturn on Capital Employed, Return on Equity, and Debt-to-Equity leverage ratio.

⚡ FINN Architecture & Cost Discipline
1. Zero-Hallucination Deterministic Math
All financial metrics — QoQ/YoY deltas, 3Y/5Y CAGRs, CFO/PAT cash conversion, Debt/Equity, Working Capital Cycle days, and Promise-vs-Delivered verdicts — are computed in Python/JS directly from reported tables, never by an LLM.
2. Three-Tier Model Routing & 10× Transcript Chunking
• Tier 0 (Regex Rules — ~90% of filings, ₹0 cost): Classifies corporate filings and scores priority.
• Tier 1 (Cheap Model — Llama-3.1-8B / Gemini Flash-Lite): Handles ambiguous filing classification, 18-word summaries, and guidance extraction on regex-filtered forward-looking concall paragraphs (cutting input tokens ~10×).
• Tier 2 (Strong Model — Llama-3.3-70B): Invoked only for Bull/Bear synthesis and free-form Q&A, receiving strictly the pre-computed JSON facts and verbatim quotes.
3. Strict Primary-Source Discipline
Pull research uses strictly NSE/BSE filings, Screener consolidated tables, Annual Reports, and Concall Transcripts. Zero broker notes, zero news commentary, and zero outside model memory.
